A LOOK INSIDE

Understanding results
is only the beginning.

V060 develops interpretation skills. V070 builds on that understanding with ten veterinary case critiques that grow in complexity.

In the first case, learners question the analysis and presentation of a canine pain study. Supplied marking criteria help trainers turn that critique into feedback.

V070 / CASE 01 / TEACHING EXAMPLE

Canine pain management

Same dogs.
Six time points.
Separate statistical tests.

What should the learner question?

Does the analysis fit an ordinal pain score? How does it handle repeated observations of the same dog? Have multiple comparisons been accounted for?

A fictional study designed for critical appraisal.
